Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions,
answered.

Here are some quick answers to common questions. Still need help? Our team is just a message away.

Do you create customised itineraries?

+
Yes, every holiday is personalised based on your interests, budget and travel style. We don't do one-size-fits-all packages.

Are your holidays suitable for families?

+
Absolutely! We design memorable experiences for couples, families, seniors and groups. Just let us know your family's needs and we'll create the perfect trip.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

+
Our travel team will get in touch with you, understand your preferences and share curated suggestions and a detailed plan — with no obligation.

Do you handle flight and visa bookings?

+
Yes, we handle all travel logistics including flights, hotels, transfers, visa guidance and travel insurance, so you can focus on enjoying your holiday.

How far in advance should I plan my trip?

+
We recommend reaching out at least 4–6 weeks before your preferred travel date. For peak seasons and international trips, earlier is always better.
